## Community charter

- This network is a professional space: we talk about careers, projects and opportunities.
- Respect for everyone. No racist, sexist, homophobic, discriminatory or hateful remarks.
- No harassment, no pushing after a refusal, no sexual solicitation.
- One profile = one real person. Impersonation is forbidden.
- No spam, abusive advertising or scams.
- Content that breaks these rules may be removed and accounts suspended.

### Report content

- Every profile and post has a “Report” link.
- Reports are read by the team, who normally reply within 7 days.
- The reported person is not told who made the report.

### Your data

- We only ask for professional information. No field about ethnic origin or skin colour.
- Your profile information is visible to members of the network.
- You can edit or delete your profile at any time from “My profile”. Deletion removes your profile, posts, comments, likes, connections and messages.
- Applicable: GDPR (EU) and nFADP (Switzerland).
- Messages can only be read by the two people in the conversation; the team has no access.
- Data is hosted by Google Firebase (database) and GitHub Pages (website). Sign-in uses a Google account.

Working version for the prototype. The final text (privacy policy, terms of use) will be drafted with a lawyer before any public launch.
